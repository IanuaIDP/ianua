These components draw the pages anyone can open without signing in: the landing page, the legal pages, the Help Center and the sign-in screens.

## Landing page

The landing page is `src/app/(public)/page.tsx`, and its parts are in `src/components/landing`. It stacks one section per block. The text lives in `messages/<language>.json` under `landing`. `src/constants/landing.ts` keeps only the ids, links, icons and order of the items, such as the questions, use cases and footer links.

| Component                          | File                                             | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `LandingHeader`                    | `header/landing-header.tsx`                      | The top bar that stays in place. It links to the page's sections, marks the one on screen (`useActiveLandingSection`), and has a phone menu and the sign-in and sign-up buttons.                                           |
| `HeroSection`                      | `hero/hero-section.tsx`                          | The first screen: the headline, the line under it, the buttons and the background picture.                                                                                                                                 |
| `ProblemSection`                   | `problem/problem-section.tsx`                    | A short statement of the problem ianua solves.                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Legal pages

These are in `src/components/legal`.

| Component                                                                                   | File               | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `LegalHeader`                                                                               | `legal-header.tsx` | The top bar of `/privacy-policy` and `/terms-of-use`, with links between them and a phone menu. The pages' text is in `messages/<language>.json` under `legal`.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `LegalSection`, `LegalParagraph`, `LegalProse`, `LegalList`, `LegalBullet`, `legalRichTags` | `legal-prose.tsx`  | The parts both legal pages are made of. `legalRichTags` are the tags used in the legal messages: `<mail>`, `<privacy>`, `<highlight>` and `<brand>`. `constants/legal.ts` keeps the contact address, the date of the last change, and the ids of the table rows and lists. A translated page says the English version applies if the two differ. |

## Help Center

The Help Center is at `/help-center/...`, and its parts are in `src/components/help-center`. It shows the articles in `src/constants/help-center.ts`. Each article is made with `article({ slug, title, lastUpdated, intro, sections, subArticles? })`. A section holds blocks of the kinds `paragraph`, `list`, `steps` or `callout`. The helpers that find articles, such as `getHelpArticleHref` and `getAdjacentHelpArticleRefs`, sit next to the content.

| Component            | File                       | What it does                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `HelpCenterHeader`   | `help-center-header.tsx`   | The top bar: logo, the path to the current article, and the phone menu.                                                                     |
| `HelpCenterSidebar`  | `help-center-sidebar.tsx`  | The tree of categories and articles, with a search filter. On a computer it is a fixed sidebar. On a phone it sits in the menu (`variant`). |
| `HelpCenterArticle`  | `help-center-article.tsx`  | One article: title, date of the last change, intro, sections and their blocks, and links to the articles before and after.                  |
| `HelpCenterToc`      | `help-center-toc.tsx`      | "On this page": the article's contents, with the part on screen marked (`useActiveHelpSection`).                                            |
| `HelpCenterFeedback` | `help-center-feedback.tsx` | "Was this helpful?" with yes and no buttons. Nothing is sent.                                                                               |

## Sign-in screens

These are in `src/components/auth`. The organization's sign-in (`/login`) and each tenant's (`/organizations/{org}/tenants/{tenant}/login`) both use them. A form knows which one it serves by whether it was given `orgSlug` and `tenantSlug`.

| Component                                                                                                                                           | File                            | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AuthLayout`                                                                                                                                        | `AuthLayout.tsx`                | The frame of the sign-in screens: top bar, background and an optional footer. It sends someone already signed in to the dashboard, unless told not to (`skipAuthenticatedRedirect`). Its Sign Up link shows only when the installation takes sign-ups (`useSignupOpen`), and a self-hosted one does not.                          |
| `AuthCard`                                                                                                                                          | `AuthCard.tsx`                  | The card in the middle that holds a form: icon, title, the line under it, and an optional "back to login" link.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `LoginForm`                                                                                                                                         | `login-form.tsx`                | Sign-in in two steps: email, then password. It offers passkeys, by button and as the browser fills in the email. On a tenant's page it also offers a mailed link, single sign-on and self sign-up, as the tenant allows. It hands over to the second-factor check or its setup when the API asks, and offers "Trust this device". |
| `MfaVerificationStep`                                                                                                                               | `mfa-verification-step.tsx`     | Asks for a 6-digit code, or a recovery code, when a sign-in needs a second factor. Works for both kinds of session.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `MfaEnrollmentStep`                                                                                                                                 | `mfa-enrollment-step.tsx`       | Shown when a tenant needs a second factor and the person has none. It shows a QR code, confirms a code, shows the recovery codes, and then opens the tenant.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `DuePasswordChangeStep`                                                                                                                             | `due-password-change-step.tsx`  | Asks for a new password when a tenant sign-in's password was set by an administrator or has expired. With a second factor it also asks for a code.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `MailedPasswordChange`                                                                                                                              | `mailed-password-change.tsx`    | Where the mailed link for such a change lands (`/change-password`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `SignupConfirmation`                                                                                                                                | `signup-confirmation.tsx`       | Where the sign-up confirmation mail lands (`/signup/confirm`). It creates the organization once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `RecoveryCodes`                                                                                                                                     | `recovery-codes.tsx`            | Shows a new second factor's recovery codes, once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `TenantSignInCompletion`                                                                                                                            | `tenant-sign-in-completion.tsx` | Finishes, exactly once, a sign-in that left the sign-in page, even when React draws it twice. Then it opens the tenant, or asks for a second factor or its setup. The two pages below share it.                                                                                                                                   |
| `MagicLinkCompletion`                                                                                                                               | `magic-link-completion.tsx`     | Where a mailed sign-in link lands. It uses the token through `TenantSignInCompletion`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `SignInConfirmation`                                                                                                                                | `sign-in-confirmation.tsx`      | Where the link that confirms a sign-in from a new device lands. It finishes the sign-in this browser kept, for an organization member or a tenant user.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `SsoCompletion`                                                                                                                                     | `sso-completion.tsx`            | Where single sign-on comes back. It trades the one-time `?code=` through `TenantSignInCompletion`. If the identity provider reported no second factor and the tenant wants one, it asks for it.                                                                                                                                   |
| `SamlBrowserStep`                                                                                                                                   | `saml-browser-step.tsx`         | The pages the platform sends a browser to for SAML: gets the signed answer and posts it to the app or identity provider. Signs Pat in first when the page needs a session.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `OAuthConsent`                                                                                                                                      | `oauth-consent.tsx`             | An app's OAuth sign-in: asks the platform what the request needs, signs Pat in or asks her to agree to the scopes, then hands the answer back to the app.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `OAuthLogout`                                                                                                                                       | `oauth-logout.tsx`              | An app's OAuth sign-out: asks to confirm when the app did not name Pat, then tells the other apps and leaves.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `OrgSsoCompletion`                                                                                                                                  | `org-sso-completion.tsx`        | Where the organization's identity provider answers. Finishes the sign-in, asking for a second factor when the organization wants one.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `WorkflowApproval`                                                                                                                                  | `workflow-approval.tsx`         | The page a chat message's Approve and Deny buttons open. Shows the data and decides it; nothing is decided by the press in the chat.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `SetPasswordForm`                                                                                                                                   | `set-password-form.tsx`         | A password and its repeat, checked against the password rules with live hints, and an optional terms box. Accepting an invitation and resetting a password use it.                                                                                                                                                                |
| `TenantSignUpForm`                                                                                                                                  | `tenant-sign-up-form.tsx`       | Self sign-up for a tenant (name, email, password) when the tenant allows it. Otherwise it says "Sign-up is closed".                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `SocialButton`                                                                                                                                      | `SocialButton.tsx`              | A provider button (Google, GitHub, passkey) with a loading state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `TokenStatusScreen`, `CheckingLinkScreen`, `ExpiredLinkScreen`, `AlreadyUsedLinkScreen`, `InvalidLinkScreen`, `ResendLinkAction`, `LinkStateAction` | `token-status-screens.tsx`      | The shared screens of mailed links (verify email, invitations, resets): checking, expired, already used and not valid, with their resend and next-step buttons.                                                                                                                                                                   |

`IntegrationConnected` (`components/auth/integration-connected.tsx`) is where a service sends the browser back after signing in. It clears the address, finishes the connection with the one-time code, then opens Connected accounts; a failure says why with Try again.
