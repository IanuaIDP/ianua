ianua is built with help from people outside the project. This page says what they get in return.

## Public credit

Everyone whose contribution is merged is named on the contributors page and in the notes of the release it ships in.

## Security hall of fame

Someone who reports a real vulnerability privately, and gives us time to fix it, is named in the security hall of fame when the fix is released. If you would rather stay anonymous, say so and we will leave your name out.

## A say in the project

Regular contributors see the roadmap early and can comment on it before it is settled. Someone who has shown good judgment over time can be invited to become a maintainer.

## A year of hosted ianua

When hosted ianua is available, the maintainers can give a contributor one year of it for free. Pat fixes a hard bug in the gateway and reviews other people's changes for a few months. The maintainers offer Pat a year of hosted ianua.

### What counts

The maintainers look at how much the work helped, not at how many changes were merged. Work that counts includes:

- a feature or a fix that took real effort and was merged,
- a reported vulnerability that was real and serious,
- a native speaker's review of a whole language,
- steady, careful review of other people's changes.

Many small changes do not add up to a year on their own.

### How it is decided

The maintainers decide together when a release ships. A maintainer does not vote on their own contribution. The decision and its reason are written down.

### The rules

- It is for the contributor's own account.
- It cannot be sold, transferred or used to run a service for anyone else.
- It ends after one year. New work can earn another year.
- It does not include paid support, and it is not payment for the work.
- It gives no rights under the [license](https://github.com/IanuaIDP/ianua/blob/main/LICENSE) beyond using the hosted service.
