A **role** is a named set of permissions. A **group** bundles roles together, so you hand them out by membership rather than one at a time.

## Roles

Open **Roles** and choose **Create Role**. Give it a name and pick its permissions.

![The Roles list](../../../../assets/guides/roles/list.png)

![The Create role form](../../../../assets/guides/roles/create.png)

Use the row's **...** menu to change a role or delete it. A deleted role can no longer be assigned.

![The Edit role form](../../../../assets/guides/roles/edit.png)

## Groups

1. Open **Groups** and choose **Create Group**.
2. Give it a name. To nest it, pick a parent: members of this group also hold everything the parent grants.

![The Create group form](../../../../assets/guides/groups/create.png)

Open a group to manage it.

- **Roles**: every member holds these roles, and so does every member of the groups beneath it.

  ![A group's Roles tab](../../../../assets/guides/groups/roles.png)

- **Members**: everyone here holds the roles this group carries, and the roles of every group above it.

  ![A group's Members tab with one member added](../../../../assets/guides/groups/members-added.png)

Deleting a group removes the grants that came with it. Nobody is deleted.
